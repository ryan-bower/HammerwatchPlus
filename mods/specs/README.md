# specs: the 4th specializations

Module id `specs` (requires `core`). Seven subclasses on the vanilla classes, each a scripted
skill set under `scripts/HWP/Specs/` with its svals under `players/hwp/specs/`. Status command:
`hwp specs`. The console and net handlers are registered in `SpecsModule.as`.

## Devil Forgemaster (Priest)

Files: `scripts/HWP/Specs/Forgemaster.as` (the skills, the channel, Heresy, the pyres),
`Forgemaster_Units.as` (the raisable units, generated by `tools/make_forgemaster_risen.py`),
`players/hwp/specs/forgemaster/*.sval`, `scripts/WorldScript/Pyre.as` (an override of the base
file, `.orig` beside it). Debug command: `hwp forge` (status line) with `body`, `raise`,
`penance`, `heresy`, `lunge`, `decay`, `cull`, `risen`, `bodies`, `trace`.

Spec (the numbers are the planner's, `planner/data/skills/priest-spec4-*.json`):

- **Call of Heresy** (passive): every enemy death on the floor rolls an intact body
  (3-10%); Deeper Depravity adds +8/+15% for an elite's body and, at epic, every boss leaves
  one (dd-boss 1.0). Heresy stacks add risen damage and the lunge chance.
- **Necrotic Resurrection**: a channel (3 s, elite 5 s, boss 10 s) under the `raising` buff
  (silence, disarm, lock-movement, lock-rotation) on a body within 150, then the host raises
  it. Mana and cooldown are spent when the channel completes. Heresy +1/+2/+3.
- **Sacrificial Penance** (the same key at max Divine Favor): the priest dodges to under the
  body, faces up and channels 3 s; Heresy -(rank table + 1 elite + 2 boss), Divine Favor spent,
  50 mana, no cooldown. The skill's HUD icon is the Penance icon while Favor is at max.
- **The channel lock** (2026-10-25): the channel locks the priest onto the body. A raise faces
  the body at its start, a Penance faces up once its dodge lands; the raising buff's
  lock-rotation holds the facing against the aim. The Dash has `ignore-blocking`, so the
  channel pins its cooldown to the remaining time (`PinDash`); a press is refused, counted
  and logged (`dash refused during <kind> channel`), and the pin is released when the channel
  ends or cancels. The Heresy lunge never rolls during a channel.
- **The channel effects**: a looping `necrotic_beam_1` (green, priest to body for a raise;
  gold and straight up out of the body for a Penance) whose width grows with the progress
  (`EffectBehavior.SetParam` each frame; a Penance's also lengthens), a pulse on the body every
  150 ms scaled by the progress (`channel_pulse.effect` for a raise, `penance_light.effect`
  for a Penance), and a progress bar with a "Raising"/"Penance" label over the priest's head
  (`RenderForgemaster`, called from `HWPSpecsModule.RenderFrame`).
- **Pyres**: using a pyre removes Pyre Sacrifice's Heresy (1/3), the brazier (class swap
  `HWPSpecsPyre`) and the large WorldScript pyre (the `Pyre.as` override) alike. A Forgemaster
  with Heresy can use either with no curse (`PyreHeresyAllows`); with neither the vanilla rule
  stands. Each use logs `<kind> pyre used[ with no curse]: heresy -N -> M`.
- **Heresy lunge / eating**, **risen decay**, **Rot Bloom**, **the cap**: see the file header
  of `Forgemaster.as`.

Test checklist (`gamelab/scenarios/`, bot `gamelab/bots/plan_forgemaster.py`):

- `plan-forgemaster.json`: the character, the binds (dd-boss 1.0 at epic), bodies, Penance,
  Heresy rules, lunge, decay.
- `plan-forgemaster-eat.json`: the eating and Penance stances.
- `plan-forgemaster-bosses.json`, `plan-forgemaster-units.json`: every raisable unit.
- `fix-forgemaster-channels.json` (2026-10-25): `channel_lock` (the Penance first, since a raise
  leaves the skill on its 20 s cooldown: dodge, lock facing up, dash refused; then a boss raise,
  10 s: facing held against a turned aim, dash refused and nothing moves, beam/pulses/bar, dash
  free after),
  `penance_icon` (raise icon below max Favor, Penance icon at max, back), `pyre_heresy`
  (brazier usable with Heresy and no curse, Heresy removed, refused with neither; the large
  pyre from the floor or a fabricated `pyre_crn_nw.pfb`), `orb_boss` (dd-boss 0 at rare, 1.0
  at epic, three boss deaths leave bodies). Status fields read: `locked`, `beam`, `pulses`,
  `lockface`, `beamframes`, `bar`, `dashpin`, `dashrefused`, `dashcd`, `icon`, `pyreuses`,
  `pyreremoved`, `pyrenocurse`.

### The risen and the bodies (the units, 2026-10-25)

`tools/make_forgemaster_risen.py` writes, per raisable enemy, a risen and a body unit into
`players/hwp/specs/forgemaster/{risen,bodies}/` plus `marker/` (gitignored, remade by
`python -m gamelab doctor --fix`); `scripts/HWP/Specs/Forgemaster_Risen.as` holds their
classes (`HWPSpecsRisenUnit`, `HWPSpecsRisenStateUnit`, `HWPSpecsBodyUnit`, subclasses of
`Forgemaster.as`'s) and `hwp risen` (each risen's movement class, speed, run-back, roaming,
search multiplier, target, distance). `python -m unittest tools.test_make_forgemaster_risen`
checks every generated unit offline.

- **A risen runs.** Every follower movement gets roaming 1.0 (an idle risen walks at full
  speed, not the half-speed amble), speed at least 4.5 (the crusader pikeman's) and run-back
  1.4x that; the target search runs on half its cooldown (`target-find-cooldown-mul` 0.5 in
  the unit and in `RisenRunAll`), with the 300 aggro range, so a risen engages what comes near
  the way the vanilla summons do. Log: `risen <id> runs: <move> speed S runback R roaming 1.0
  search-mul 0.5`.
- **A risen looks like a summon:** the `npc` material (the green silhouette through walls the
  crusaders have, not the enemies' red), the `npc_normal` minimap dot, and an elite's ring from
  `marker/` in the summon HP bar's orange (255 84 0: `GetHPBarColor` case 5 is vec4(1, 0.33, 0),
  the "yellow" the user sees on the bar; the enemies' ring is 255 37 37).
- **A body is dead:** grey (`BODY_TINT` 0.45), `npc-noxray` (no silhouette), no minimap dot,
  no ring, every sprite frozen on its first frame, no effects, lights or particles (a Dark
  Citadel wisp, effects only, shows the still grey `dead_core.png` orb), and every collision
  shape shoot-, aim- and charge-through, so the Priest's beam and any shot pass it. Log:
  `body <id> <source> tint grey`.
- **Floors:** the game carries every saveable summon through a floor change itself
  (PlayerRecord.Save's `summons`, re-produced by Player's constructor): the lab could not
  reproduce the loss (fix-specs-risen-floors 20261025-181000: two risen brutes at 60% came
  through, `floor restore: 2 in the snapshot, 2 already live, 0 produced`). What it did show is
  what shrinks them on arrival: the decay (1% per 3 s, also over the loading screen) and the new
  floor's enemies at the stairs (one brute read 0.407 after 2.5 s among 25 bandits). The safety
  net stays: `mods/summons` tracks the risen producers (prefix
  `players/hwp/specs/forgemaster/risen/`, setting `summons.risen`), snapshots them twice a
  second and produces any shortfall on the next floor, logging the restore line; a user report
  should come with that line and whether "Exception when saving summons" is in the log (the
  catch in PlayerRecord.Save that would drop every summon at once).
- Scenarios: `fix-specs-risen-speed.json` (`risen_speed`), `fix-specs-risen-look.json`
  (`risen_look`, with a shot of the wisp body for the eye), `fix-specs-body-passthrough.json`
  (`body_passthrough`: the scepter's 65 px hitscan and the lantern's 160 px heal beam, each
  proven on the bare lane first, then through a body 22 px out), `fix-specs-risen-floors.json`
  (`risen_floors`, mods core, classes, specs, summons). The mission start is a corridor, so the
  bot's `lanes()` reads `gl terrain` and puts every priest, brute, body and target on the
  longest free lane (20261025-180859: a priest teleported 260 px east stood in rock, as did
  both brutes, and nothing could path or see anything).
